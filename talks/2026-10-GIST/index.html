[12:35–14:35, 120 seconds] Equal feature widths do not mean equal resources: the direct and parent presentations have 3L versus 35L Gaussian amplitudes, plus shared frequency/phase parameters. Here the active draft reports ensemble-correlation evaluation costs, not forward milliseconds per sampled network. The baseline direct time is one for each workload. First: complete production and calibration gives 10.2481. Advance 1: retaining amplitude dependence for 1000 repeated calls gives 72.0258. Advance 2: combining fixed-amplitude coefficients in advance reduces the ratio to 1.2743. The three tasks have different construction, integration and reuse costs. Ratios alone do not establish matched-accuracy savings: neither complete task attained the prescribed 1% target for the full correlation vector. The finite-width bias, estimator variance and calibration generalization are separate questions. The practical conclusion is to choose a presentation and prior for a declared observable, error measure and workload.
