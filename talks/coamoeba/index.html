What is a Neural-Network QFT and Why Do We Care?

QFT: Generating functional

\[ Z[J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi\, e^{-S[\phi] + \int d^Dx\, \phi(x)J(x)} \]

Key Idea: Approximate the functional integral using UATs and neural networks.

[Halverson, Maiti and Stoner; 2008]

Neural networks come with a set of parameters, e.g. sigmoids \(\sigma(a \cdot x + b)\).

Integrate over these parameters with a measure:

\[ \int \mathcal{D}\phi\, e^{-S[\phi]} \longleftrightarrow \lim_{N\rightarrow\infty} \int \prod_i^N d\theta_i\, P(\theta_i) \]

In the limit where the UAT applies, both sides become equal.

Result: Novel way of constructing QFT correlators.

What About Neural-Network CFTs?

CFT: More symmetry \(\rightarrow\) more constrained dynamics.

Full CFT data encoded in \((\Delta_i, \lambda_{ij})\).

NN-CFT: Approximation is exact at any number of neurons. No UAT limit required.

[Halverson, Naskar, Tian; 2024]

Concept Field Theory Neural Network Theory
Conformal Primary \(\phi(x) \mapsto \lambda^{-\Delta}\phi(x)\) \(\Phi(X) \mapsto \lambda^{-\Delta}\Phi(X)\)
