About the database

The Brane Tiling Database is a searchable catalogue of four-dimensional N=1 quiver gauge theories engineered by D3-branes probing toric Calabi–Yau threefold singularities. Records bring together, where available, toric phases, quivers, superpotentials, R-charges, brane tilings, perfect matchings, and Seiberg-duality data.
